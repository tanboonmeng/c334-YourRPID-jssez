One legal threshold to note
Manufacturers with shareholders' funds of RM2.5 million or more, or 75 or more full-time employees, are required to apply for a manufacturing licence through MIDA, according to the playbook.

Before you count on an incentive

A

Match the operating model

Singapore and Malaysia have different rules and conditions. Incentives should follow the operating model, not dictate it. Smaller investments may still access Malaysia's standard investment incentives.

Playbook, p. 15
B

Track cross-border workdays

Working in Johor may create personal tax, reporting or work-pass obligations. Track where employees work, what they do and how long they stay. Keep records for any treaty claim.

Playbook, p. 17
C

Document related-party pricing

Set fair, arm's-length prices for transactions between your Singapore and Johor entities, and keep records to support them.

Playbook, p. 14

04 / Plan for your people

The right pass
for the right work.

The appendix describes several routes for foreign professionals and business travellers. The pass needed depends on the purpose and type of activity.

EMPLOYMENT PASS

Expatriate posts

MIDA evaluates expatriate-post applications in manufacturing, selected services and approved regional establishments or offices.

  • Key Post: permanent, high-level managerial role.
  • Term Post: temporary role for a specific period, requiring relevant qualifications, experience or expertise.

SMF Playbook, p. 28

PROFESSIONAL VISIT PASS

Temporary expertise

For specific professional work such as training, consultancy or machine installation on behalf of an overseas company.

  • Up to 12 months and not extendable.
  • Does not provide long-term employment rights or immigration privileges.

SMF Playbook, p. 28

INVESTOR PASS

Business travellers

For eligible foreign business travellers entering Malaysia for business under a multi-entry facility.

  • Up to 12 months: six months at a time, with an option to extend for a further six months, subject to justification and requirements.
  • Includes new, pipeline and existing investors.

SMF Playbook, p. 29

Cross-border mobility does not remove tax or immigration obligations. The playbook advises companies to assess work activities and time spent in Johor. SMF Playbook, p. 17
